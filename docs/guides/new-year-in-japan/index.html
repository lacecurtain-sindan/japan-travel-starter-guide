Visiting Japan over New Year? Read this first

Updated November 2026

Short answerJapan does not fully shut down, but from roughly December 29 to January 3 many attractions, shops, restaurants, banks and ATMs close on one or more days, especially January 1. Trains and hotels are busiest around Dec 29 to 31 and Jan 2 to 4.

What usually closes

  • Many museums and some attractions, on part or all of the holiday
  • Small independent restaurants and shops
  • Banks, government offices and some ATMs

Closure dates differ by place and by year. Check the 2026/27 opening days for every place that matters to your plan.

What stays open

  • Convenience stores
  • Major shrines and temples, which are very crowded for hatsumode, the first shrine visit of the year
  • Many large chains and big department stores, often with shorter hours

When travel is busiest

Japanese residents leave big cities around December 29 to 31 and return around January 2 to 4. Book trains and hotels for those days early.

A simple plan

  1. Book your hotel now. Prices rise and rooms go fast over New Year.
  2. Keep January 1 light: a shrine visit and a walk instead of museums.
  3. Carry some cash in case ATMs near you are closed.
