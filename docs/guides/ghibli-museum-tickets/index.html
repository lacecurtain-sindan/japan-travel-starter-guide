How to get Ghibli Museum tickets (they sell out fast)

Updated November 2026

This guide contains affiliate links. If you book through them, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. How this works

Short answerYou cannot buy tickets at the door. Tickets go on sale on Lawson Ticket at 10:00 Japan time on the 10th of each month, for visits in the following month. Visiting in January? Buy on December 10.

When tickets go on sale

If you visit inBuy on (10:00 Japan time)
January 2027December 10, 2026
February 2027January 10, 2027
March 2027February 10, 2027

10:00 in Japan is 01:00 GMT the same day, and 20:00 New York time the evening before.

What to do before sale day

  1. Create a Lawson Ticket account a few days early. Do not leave it for the morning of the sale.
  2. Decide your date and a backup date.
  3. Pick your entry time in advance. You choose a time slot when you buy.

On sale day

  1. Log in about 10 minutes before 10:00 Japan time.
  2. Go straight to the Ghibli Museum page and choose your date and time without hesitating.
  3. Complete payment and keep your confirmation.

If it sells out

  • Try other dates in the same month, especially weekdays.
  • Check again later: some tickets become available after the first rush.
  • Already in Japan? Tickets are also sold on Loppi machines in Lawson stores, which require a Japanese phone number. See eSIM vs pocket WiFi.
