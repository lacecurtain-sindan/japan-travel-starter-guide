Japan tax-free shopping changed on November 1, 2026

Updated November 2026

Short answer

From November 1, 2026 you pay the full price including 10% tax at the store, then get the tax back as a refund after customs checks your items when you leave Japan. Keep receipts and items together, and do not use tax-free snacks or cosmetics before you fly.

How to get your refund

  1. Show your passport when you buy, at a shop that offers tax-free sales.
  2. Keep the items and receipts together. Do not open or use consumables such as snacks, drinks or cosmetics.
  3. When you leave Japan, go to customs before you check in for your flight and show your passport and the items.
  4. After customs confirms the purchase, the shop sends the refund.

Tips that save you trouble

  • Using the automated gates when you arrive? Ask an officer for a passport entry stamp right afterwards.
  • Leave extra time at the airport. Queues are likely while everyone gets used to the new system.
  • Pack tax-free items where you can reach them, not deep in checked luggage.
  • Buying something big? Some shops can ship it overseas directly, which still works under the new rules.
