Tokyo winter illuminations 2026: 5 spots and dates

Updated November 2026

Short answer

For an easy first night, walk the Marunouchi lights next to Tokyo Station (free, Nov 12 to Feb 14). In Roppongi, combine Keyakizaka (until Dec 25) with Tokyo Midtown (Nov 26 to Dec 25). Shibuya's Blue Cave runs for only 17 days, Dec 11 to 27. For a full evening out, Yomiuriland is lit until April 4, 2027 (park admission needed).

1. Marunouchi: the easy one

The trees along Marunouchi Naka-dori are lit from November 12, 2026 to February 14, 2027, 16:00 to 23:00, and until midnight in December. It is free and one minute from the Marunouchi South Exit of Tokyo Station, so it works even on the evening you arrive.

2. Roppongi Hills Keyakizaka

About 970,000 LEDs line Keyakizaka-dori from November 5 to December 25, 17:00 to 22:00. The Roppongi Hills Christmas market runs nearby from November 21 to December 25, 11:00 to 21:00.

3. Tokyo Midtown

Also in Roppongi, within walking distance of Roppongi Hills. The garden lights run from November 26 to December 25, 17:00 to 23:00, and are free. The seasonal ice rink is paid. Doing Keyakizaka and Midtown on the same evening is easy.

4. Shibuya Blue Cave

About 500,000 blue lights from Shibuya Koen-dori to the zelkova trees of Yoyogi Park, December 11 to 27, 17:00 to 22:00. It is a short run, so plan for it if your trip falls in those dates.

5. Yomiuriland Jewellumination

A large light show inside the Yomiuriland amusement park in west Tokyo, from October 29, 2026 to April 4, 2027. The lights come on around 16:00. There is no separate illumination ticket: park admission covers it, and a pass with rides costs more. The park has irregular closing days, so check its calendar before you go. Access: the Sky Shuttle gondola from Keio-Yomiuri-Land Station, or a bus from Yomiuriland-mae Station.
