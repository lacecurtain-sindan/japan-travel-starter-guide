Where to stay in Tokyo on your first trip

Short answer

First trip and want easy transport? Shinjuku. Want a traditional feel and an easy ride from Narita? Ueno or Asakusa. Planning day trips by bullet train? Tokyo Station. Here for shopping and nightlife? Shibuya.

The four areas compared

AreaWhy stay hereBest for
ShinjukuHuge transport hub, endless food, open lateFirst-timers who want everything nearby
ShibuyaShopping, nightlife, young energy, city viewsShoppers and night owls
Ueno / AsakusaTemples, old-town streets, direct Skyliner from NaritaNarita arrivals, traditional atmosphere
Tokyo Station / GinzaBullet trains, polished hotels, central locationDay trips to Kyoto, Nikko or Mt Fuji areas

How to choose in 30 seconds

  • Landing at Narita with big suitcases: Ueno or Asakusa (see Narita to Tokyo).
  • Using a JR Pass for day trips: Tokyo Station.
  • Want food and transport options at any hour: Shinjuku.
  • Traveling for shopping and nightlife: Shibuya.

Tips before you book

  • Stay within a 5 to 10 minute walk of a station. It matters more than the exact area.
  • Tokyo hotel rooms are small. Check the room size if you travel with large luggage.
  • Book early for cherry blossom season and New Year, when prices rise.
