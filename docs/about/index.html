About

Japan Travel Starter Guide helps first-time visitors handle the practical side of a Japan trip: airport transfers, internet, tickets that sell out, where to stay, and seasonal surprises.

The guides are written in Tokyo, Japan, by a team that reads Japanese sources and official pages directly, so you get the details that English summaries often miss.

Every guide is checked against official sources when it is written. Prices and rules in Japan do change, so each page shows when it was last reviewed. If you spot something out of date, we want to hear about it.
